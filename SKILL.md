## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one very small, uncoloured line vignette — the subject plus only the few small things right beside it — drawn in one black soft-pencil line of a single weight, centred slightly above the middle of a wide, clean white (barely warm or cool) page that is mostly empty, with generous headroom and no stroke touching any edge or the fold. Everything is seen front-on or in straight side view, squared off and a little wonky, proportions slightly squashed like a toy model; openings become small squares, textures a few short strokes, details shrink to tiny icons, and there is no shading, hatching, grey fill or colour. The group stands on a short broken horizontal ground line that runs out a little to each side and fades, and one fluffy cloud, taller than the subject and drawn with the sparsest outline, rises behind it on one side. When the photograph is a scene, keep only its most representative single object or corner. The one short line of text is a loose handwritten here-and-now poem in the same pen, small, centred below the vignette after a large gap, naming one thing really visible in the photo. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled or enlarged vignette, a missing cloud or ground line, a subject floating without footing, any colour, grey blocks or hatched shadows, strong-perspective realistic architecture, fussy ornament, and copied signage.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-352/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
